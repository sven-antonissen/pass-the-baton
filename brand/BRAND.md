# Pass the Baton: brand guide

## Why

**What applies to the people should apply to those who lead them.**

All over the world, people hand their work over to the next generation at a certain age. Pass the Baton asks leaders to do the same: anyone who has reached the statutory retirement age of their own country, and at the latest 67, does not start a new term in one of the highest offices. We don't ask this because we distrust older people. We ask it out of respect for renewal. Passing the baton is an act of leadership, not a defeat.

## Tone of voice

We sound like a good relay coach: calm, clear, warm, and sure of the next step.

| Do | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **State the rule plainly.** One idea per sentence. | "Same age norm for leaders as for the people they serve." |
| **Honour experience.** Older people are mentors, not targets. | "Your experience doesn't disappear. Pass it on as adviser, mentor, ambassador." |
| **Invite action.** Speak in verbs and give people a gesture they can make. | "Hand the baton to someone today. Post it with #PassTheBaton." |

| Don't | Example to avoid |
| --- | --- |
| **Mock age** or bodies, and don't use jokes about frailty. | ~~"Too old to lead."~~ |
| **Take sides.** No party, country, leader or ideology is singled out. | ~~"Throw out [leader's name]!"~~ |
| **Shout or threaten.** We persuade; we never use force. | ~~"Drag them out of office."~~ |

## Taglines

1. **Passing it on is leadership.**
2. Same age. Same rule.
3. Make room for renewal.
4. What applies to the people applies to those who lead.
5. Your turn to hand it on.

## Colour palette

The brand runs on one signature colour: **Baton Amber**. It is the colour of the baton in every logo, illustration and object. Amber is never mixed with other accent colours. It always sits on neutral Ink or Paper, which keeps the movement free of any party, national or religious colour scheme. Local chapters may not recolour the baton.

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relay Ink | `#111318` | Main text, wordmark, dark backgrounds. 16.8:1 on Paper. |
| Paper | `#F6F3EC` | Main light background; text on Ink and Amber Shadow. |
| Baton Amber | `#F5A623` | Signature baton metal (mid-tone), highlights, buttons with Ink text (9.2:1). Never use it for text on Paper. |
| Amber Glint | `#FFF4DC` | Specular highlight and highlight line on the baton; a light tint for panels with Ink text (17:1). |
| Amber Shade | `#A85F07` | Core shade of the baton cylinder and the inner rim. Graphic only, never text. |
| Amber Shadow | `#6B3B04` | Shadow edge of the baton; small accent text on Paper (8.4:1, AA). |

**Baton gradient** (across the width, top to bottom, light from the upper left): `#D98E1C` 0%, `#FFCF73` 10%, `#FFF4DC` 20% (specular), `#FFC34F` 30%, `#F5A623` 55%, `#C27A0C` 78%, `#A85F07` 88%, `#B5700F` 94% (reflected light), `#6B3B04` 100%.

Accessibility: body text is always Ink on Paper/Glint/Amber, or Paper on Ink. Meaning never depends on colour: the baton's shape and the wordmark carry the message.

## Typography

Both typefaces are free on Google Fonts.

- **Headings and wordmark: Archivo Black.** Bold, wide and sporty. H1 56–72 px (posters 160 pt+), H2 36 px, H3 24 px. Tracking −1% on large sizes. All caps only for short labels.
- **Body: Inter** (Regular 400, SemiBold 600). Body 18 px / line height 1.55, small print 14 px, labels 13 px in all caps with +12% tracking.
- For scripts other than Latin, pair with **Noto Sans** (matching script) at the same sizes. The logo symbol works without any text.

## Logo

Every logo is built around the same lifelike Baton Amber baton. The baton is always rising from lower left to upper right: forward motion, a hand-off in progress.

- **logo-1: Horizontal lockup.** The baton at a rising 32° angle with a soft cast shadow, followed by the wordmark "Pass the Baton" in Archivo Black, with the tagline beneath. This is the primary logo for the website header, letterheads, banners and the sign-up page.
- **logo-2: The Disc (emblem).** A strong Ink disc with the baton crossing it at 40°, the open rim facing you. It reads from across a square and at avatar size. Use it for social avatars, stickers, pins, placards and app icons. `favicon.svg` is its 32 px simplification: still round, shaded and with the open rim.
- **logo-3: Stacked lockup.** A wide baton floating at a slight 14° angle above "PASS THE / BATON". Use it for posters, T-shirts, stage backdrops and square or vertical formats.

**Clear space:** at least the baton's diameter on all sides. **Minimum size:** lockups 160 px wide; the Disc 24 px.

**One colour and black and white:** the baton becomes a solid silhouette with the open rim knocked out as an ellipse, and the highlight line drawn as a thin reverse line. The silhouette alone stays recognisable. Do not stretch, outline or recolour the baton, and don't put it on busy photos without the Disc.

## The baton

The baton is the heart of the brand and always looks like a real relay baton: a smooth, hollow aluminium tube, 30 cm × 4 cm (7.5 : 1).

**How it is drawn**

- **Shape:** a cylinder seen in slight perspective. The near end is a full ellipse (horizontal radius about 0.35 × tube radius). The body tapers about 10% towards the far end, which closes with a half-ellipse.
- **Open rim:** the near end is always open. An outer ellipse in a light-to-shade rim gradient, an inner darker ellipse (`#1A0E02` to `#9A5A0A`) showing the lit inner wall, and a thin Glint highlight on the upper-left lip.
- **Lighting:** one key light from the upper left at about 45°. The specular band sits at 20% of the width, the darkest core at about 88%, then a faint reflected light, then the shadow edge.
- **Finish:** anodised Baton Amber with a subtle brushed texture of fine lengthwise lines (white and dark at about 10% opacity), plus one crisp Glint highlight line running the full length.
- **Angle:** always rising left to right, between 10° and 45°. Hero 24°, horizontal logo 32°, Disc 40°, stacked 14°.
- **Shadow:** wherever it sits above a surface, a soft blurred ellipse in Ink at 25–35% opacity, darker and tighter under the end nearest the ground.

**Where it appears**

- **Online:** the Disc as an avatar, a profile-frame overlay with the baton crossing the corner, and short loops of one hand passing the baton to another, all tagged #PassTheBaton and local variants such as #StokjeDoorgeven. The hero `baton.svg` and `handover.svg` open the website.
- **Posters:** one big baton on Paper or Ink, a single tagline in Archivo Black, the local retirement age and the sign-up link. Lots of empty space.
- **Physical object:** a real 30 cm aluminium relay baton, anodised in Baton Amber, with "Pass the Baton" laser-etched small near the far end. On the yearly action day, people pass it on hand to hand in squares, at work and on camera.
- **With athletes:** relay athletes show the real hand-off: the receiver's open palm, the giver's firm push. A duo of one older and one younger person passing the baton is the signature image. It shows renewal, never young against old.
