# Code of Conduct

This movement is about respect between generations. That starts here.

## We do

- Treat everyone with respect, whatever their age, origin, language, beliefs or political preference.
- Argue about ideas, never attack people.
- Assume good intentions and ask before we judge.
- Keep in mind that some participants live in countries where taking part carries real risk. We protect their privacy.

## We don't

- Harass, insult, threaten or discriminate.
- Mock older or younger people. This is not young against old.
- Share anyone's personal information without permission.
- Call for or glorify violence.
- Use this project for party politics or personal gain.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers by opening a confidential issue or contacting them directly. Maintainers may remove contributions and exclude people who break these rules. Reports are handled discreetly.
