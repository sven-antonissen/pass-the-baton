## 2026-10-10 (review of pull request #1)

- Make the brand **visually strong**: bold, confident, recognisable at a glance.
- **Always show a lifelike baton**: a real, round, shaded metal relay baton with an open rim, in every logo and illustration. The flat bars and lines of the first proposal are not enough.
- Keep the name "Pass the Baton" and the wordmark.
- The baton has one signature colour, used consistently in every logo (the first proposal mixed amber and dark ink).
