# China (CN): no outreach recommended

Status: **draft for maintainer review. Nothing has been sent.**
Researched: 2026-10-13.

## Recommendation: caution instead of outreach

I am **not proposing any organisations or draft e-mails** for China.

The reasons:

- Independent civil-society groups, student groups and anything resembling political advocacy in mainland China are widely reported to operate under heavy restrictions. A demand about the age of top office-holders is political by nature and touches the highest leadership directly.
- A message from a foreign movement, even a polite and non-partisan one, could expose the recipients to questioning or other consequences. They would carry that risk, not us.
- Organisations that are legally registered and publicly active in the country are mostly state-linked or state-supervised (for example, official youth or elderly federations). Those would count as government-linked bodies, which the partner rules exclude.
- I did not run searches for China in this run, because there is no list I could recommend contacting safely.

## Suggestions for maintainers

- Do not contact organisations or individuals in mainland China by e-mail or social media on the movement's behalf.
- Do not publish or share names of anyone in China who shows interest in the movement, and do not ask for them.
- If someone in China contacts the movement first, reply only through the channel they used, keep it general, never ask for personal details and let them decide how visible they want to be.
- Consider whether the movement's website, sign-up form and social accounts should carry a short privacy note for visitors from countries where activism carries risk.
- If the maintainers still want to explore China later, a safer route is to look at independent academic or diaspora organisations **outside** the country that already write about ageing, leadership and governance. That needs a separate, deliberate decision and fresh research, and it should not be done from this draft.
- A short statement on the China country page that explains why there is no local outreach could be considered. It should avoid anything that could be read as an accusation against individuals.

## Things to check

- Whether the country profile `data/countries/cn.json` is accurate and neutral in tone, since it may be read inside China as well.
- Whether the maintainers' own security practices (shared inboxes, document sharing) are suitable before any contact with people in a high-risk country, even if it is only a reply to an inbound message.
