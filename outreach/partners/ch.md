## Draft e-mail (English)

**Subject:** Pass the Baton: would you like to talk?

Dear [organisation name] team,

I am writing on behalf of Pass the Baton, a global, non-violent and non-partisan movement. Our one demand is simple: anyone who has reached the statutory retirement age of their own country, and at the latest 67, should not start a new term in one of the highest offices. A term already under way may be completed. We are not against older people. We believe that passing the baton is an act of leadership.

You can read our manifesto here: https://pass-the-baton.org/manifesto/

We know your organisation works on [youth participation / political renewal, adjust per organisation]. We are not assuming that you share our view, and we are not asking you to take a position. We would just like to know whether you would be open to a short conversation.

If this is not relevant for you, no problem at all, and thank you for reading.

With kind regards,

[name], Pass the Baton

## Check before contacting

- Find and confirm the current contact page or general address of each organisation on its own website. None was verified in this run apart from Operation Libero's contact page.
- Operation Libero calls itself a political movement and campaigns on issues. Its contact page also uses sharply political language. Check carefully whether contact fits the movement's non-partisan principle before writing. If in doubt, skip it.
- Switzerland has no single head of state or government. The Federal Council is a collective body, and the presidency rotates every year. Check `data/countries/ch.json` and adapt the local framing (for example, how the demand applies to Federal Council members) before using the text above.
- Check the retirement age used in the Swiss profile, and whether the draft should also mention French and Italian-speaking audiences.
