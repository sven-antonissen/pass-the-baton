## Before contacting (for the maintainer)

- **Vlaamse Jeugdraad** describes itself as the official advisory body of the Flemish Government. It is an advisory council, not a ministry, but please decide whether it falls under the rule "no government bodies". Its own pages also say its advisers represent the council and not a party, which fits our non-partisan stance.
- **G1000** pursues its own campaign (a permanent Citizens' Council in the Senate). Check that approaching it does not look like taking a side in that debate.
- **VVS and FEF** are student unions with elected boards that change every year. Check the current contact details and the board's term before writing.
- **Language:** use Dutch for Flemish organisations and French for FEF. Do not write to a Flemish body in French or the reverse.
- **Gaps:** no civic-education group, associations of older people or French- or German-speaking youth council were verified in this run. Candidates worth a follow-up search (not verified, not included): the French-speaking Conseil de la Jeunesse (it appears to be in transition under a 2019 decree, so check whether it is active), the Vlaamse Scholierenkoepel (secondary-school pupils), and the Jugendrat of the German-speaking Community.
