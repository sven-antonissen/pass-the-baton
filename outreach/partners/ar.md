## Draft e-mail (English)

**Subject:** Would you like to talk? "Pass the Baton" and a retirement-age norm for top leaders

Dear [organisation name] team,

I am writing on behalf of Pass the Baton, a global, non-violent and non-partisan movement. Our demand is short: anyone who has reached the statutory retirement age of their own country, and at the latest 67, does not start a new term in one of the highest offices. A term already under way may be completed. The norm would apply to everyone, in every country and to every party.

We are not against older people. We believe renewal at the top is a matter of equality and of respect for the generations who will live with today's decisions. Our manifesto is here: https://pass-the-baton.org/manifesto/

We do not assume that this fits your mission, and we are not asking for any endorsement. We would simply like to know whether you would be open to a short conversation, and to hear your honest view, including any objections.

Thank you for your time and for your work.

Kind regards,
[name], Pass the Baton

## Before contacting (maintainer checklist)

- Have a native Spanish speaker review the Spanish draft. Check whether the manifesto has a Spanish version; the link points to the English one.
- Check `data/countries/ar.json` and make sure the draft matches it. If a local retirement age is mentioned, it must match the profile.
- Use only the public routes above: the Poder Ciudadano contact form and the general ACIJ address. For the other three, find the general contact form or address on the site. Do not use staff addresses. A third-party project page lists named staff e-mails for OAJNU; they were deliberately not collected.
- FUA: it describes itself as plural, but student federations are often tied to party-affiliated groups. Check how it positions itself so that the non-partisan principle is kept. Confirm it is currently active and who handles general enquiries.
- Directorio Legislativo: confirm the exact contact page. Its fit is weaker, so consider contacting it later.
- Argentina is a democracy and no particular risk for this kind of outreach was identified in this run. Keep the tone strictly non-partisan, because age and office-holding are politically sensitive topics.
