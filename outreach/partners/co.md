## Caution: activism risk

Colombia is a democracy with an active civil society, and writing to a transparency NGO or a university programme about an age limit carries little risk in itself. Still, social leaders, community activists and some student groups face threats and violence in parts of the country. Please:

- keep early contact to formal, organisation-level channels;
- do not push local groups to stage public actions without their own security assessment;
- keep the message strictly non-partisan. Colombian politics is polarised and the movement should not be read as aimed at any one leader or camp.

A term already under way may be completed. We are not against older people. Experience stays valuable as advice and mentoring. We ask for the same age norm for leaders as for the citizens they serve.

## Before contacting: maintainer checks

- **Fill in the placeholders** (`[organisation]`, the personalised sentence) and only use the general contact routes above. Do not use personal addresses. A UNCAC Coalition page lists a named person's e-mail for Congreso Visible. I deliberately did not copy it.
- **Transparencia por Colombia:** open the site and confirm the current general contact form or address. It is a Transparency International chapter and counts companies among its institutional members. Check whether it has a policy on taking positions outside its anti-corruption remit.
- **Congreso Visible** is a programme of a private university, not a government body. Confirm with the contact page whether the team answers requests that are not about Congress.
- Check the retirement age in `data/countries/co.json` before putting any number into a message. I left it out on purpose.
- Neither organisation has been shown to support the movement. The drafts say so.
