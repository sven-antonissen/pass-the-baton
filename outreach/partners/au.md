## Draft e-mail (English)

**Subject:** Would you like to talk? "Pass the Baton" and a retirement-age norm for top leaders

Dear [organisation name] team,

I am writing on behalf of Pass the Baton, a global, non-violent and non-partisan movement. Our demand is short: anyone who has reached the statutory retirement age of their own country, and at the latest 67, does not start a new term in one of the highest offices. A term already under way may be completed. The norm would apply to everyone, in every country and to every party.

We are not against older people. We believe renewal at the top is a matter of equality and of respect for the generations who will live with today's decisions. Our manifesto is here: https://pass-the-baton.org/manifesto/

We do not assume that this fits your mission, and we are not asking for any endorsement. We would simply like to know whether you would be open to a short conversation, and to hear your honest view, including any objections.

Thank you for your time and for your work.

Kind regards,
[name], Pass the Baton

(English is the main language in Australia, so no second version is needed.)

## Before contacting (maintainer checklist)

- **Add more candidates before outreach.** Suggested leads to verify on their own sites in a later run (none were verified here and none should be contacted on this basis): a national student union (e.g. the National Union of Students), the Foundation for Young Australians, Transparency International Australia, a national association of older people (e.g. COTA Australia), and youth civic-education groups. Check each is active.
- Use only public routes: the AYAC contact page and general address, and the general contact forms or addresses of the other two. A third-party directory lists a media address for ADN; confirm any address on ADN's own site. Do not collect staff names or personal addresses.
- AYAC has worked with the Australian Government on a youth steering committee. That does not rule it out, but check how it handles non-partisan, independent positions.
- The Centre for Public Integrity and ADN focus on integrity and campaign finance. The age norm is outside their stated topics, so a request for a conversation is more appropriate than a request for support.
- Australia is a democracy and no particular risk for this kind of outreach was identified in this run. Keep the tone strictly non-partisan.
