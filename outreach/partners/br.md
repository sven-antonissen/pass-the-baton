# Brazil: potential partner organisations

*Draft prepared 2026-10-10 by the partners agent. **Nothing has been sent.** A maintainer reviews and decides whether and how to make contact.*

**Note on coverage:** only four organisations could be verified from their own websites in this run (the target is five to eight). Unverified candidates are named under "Before contacting" for a follow-up search.

**Timing:** Brazil holds general elections in October 2026 (to my knowledge the second round is on 25 October; I did not verify this in this run). The movement's demand concerns age norms for top offices, and in an election period it can easily be read as being about a particular candidate. A maintainer should consider waiting until after the election.

## Draft e-mail (English)

**Subject:** Introducing Pass the Baton: would you like to talk?

Dear [organisation] team,

I am writing on behalf of Pass the Baton, a global, non-violent and non-partisan movement. Its demand is simple: anyone who has reached the statutory retirement age of their own country, and at the latest 67, does not start a new term in one of the highest offices. A term already under way may be completed. We are not against older people. We ask that leaders follow the same age norm as the citizens they serve. The norm is meant to apply to everyone, in every country, and does not target any person or party.

Our manifesto is here: https://pass-the-baton.org/manifesto/

## Before contacting (for the maintainer)

- **UNE (strong caution):** it describes itself as the national student representative, but its own site has carried material about re-electing the current president, and press and encyclopaedia sources describe long-standing leadership ties to a political party. The site snippet seen in this run may be old. Because our movement is strictly non-partisan, consider whether to contact UNE at all; if so, wait until after the election and read the current site first.
- **Politize!** is explicitly non-partisan and mentions "renovação política". The contact address appears on its own pages, but the `/contato/` page is mostly an FAQ page. Check which channel it currently prefers.
- **Transparência Brasil** says it does not handle individual complaints and focuses on public transparency; the demand is outside its usual scope. Keep the message short and make no claim about a fit.
- **Transparência Internacional Brasil** is part of an international network; check whether it takes positions on its own or only after the network does.
- **Gaps:** no independent youth council, associations of older people or political-renewal group was verified in this run. The national youth council (CONJUVE) is a government body and is excluded by our rules. Candidates for a follow-up search (not verified, not included): Movimento Acredito, Movimento Voto Consciente, RenovaBR, Open Knowledge Brasil. Check that none of these is closely tied to candidates or parties before listing it.
