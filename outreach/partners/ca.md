## Draft e-mail (English)

Canada's main language is English. If a maintainer writes to Apathy is Boring or the CFS in French, the same text can be translated. I have not drafted a French version.

**Subject:** Pass the Baton: would you like to talk?

Dear [organisation name] team,

I am writing on behalf of Pass the Baton, a global, non-violent and non-partisan movement. Our one demand is simple: anyone who has reached the statutory retirement age of their own country, and at the latest 67, should not start a new term in one of the highest offices. A term already under way may be completed. We are not against older people. We believe that passing the baton is an act of leadership.

You can read our manifesto here: https://pass-the-baton.org/manifesto/

We know your organisation works on [democracy / youth engagement / civic education, adjust per organisation]. We are not assuming that you share our view, and we are not asking you to take a position. We would just like to know whether you would be open to a short conversation, or whether there is a way our two efforts could learn from each other.

If this is not relevant for you, no problem at all, and thank you for reading.

With kind regards,

[name], Pass the Baton

## Check before contacting

- Confirm each organisation's current contact page on its own website. Only the CFS contact page was verified in this run.
- Samara and Apathy is Boring are charities and describe themselves as non-partisan. Do not suggest that the movement is aimed at any Canadian party or leader.
- The CFS runs advocacy campaigns on education policy. Check that it is comfortable engaging with a cross-cutting, non-partisan ask. Provincial components (for example CFS-Ontario) have their own contact details, so use the national address first.
- Springtide's own site could not be fully verified. Its legal filings suggest it is active, but confirm before writing.
- Check the Canada profile in `data/countries/ca.json` for the statutory retirement age used in the local framing.
