## Caution first

Outreach in Bangladesh should not go ahead until a maintainer has made a risk assessment, ideally with advice from someone with local knowledge.

- Civil society in Bangladesh has operated under political pressure. For example, Transparency International Bangladesh criticised government rules on foreign-funded NGOs in 2016 (see https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transparency_International_Bangladesh). The political situation may have changed since then, and I could not assess today's conditions in this run.
- A demand about the age of top office holders is political by nature, even though the movement is non-partisan. Contact could expose an organisation, its staff or its volunteers to risk, and a first e-mail may be read by others.
- I found only **two** organisations that I could verify in this run, below the target of five to eight. I left out anything I could not confirm, so this list is incomplete. More research is needed, ideally with local advice, before any outreach.

## Maintainer checks before contacting

- **Risk first.** Decide whether to make contact at all. Consider local advice, a secure channel, and what happens to the organisation if the message becomes public. If in doubt, do not send.
- The country profile (`data/countries/bd.json`) was not part of this run's input. Check the retirement age and the current political situation yourself.
- Only two organisations were verified, so the list is short on purpose. Please do not fill the gap with unverified names. Candidates for a later run: youth, student and civic education groups, once their sites can be checked.
- Confirm that SHUJAN and TIB are still active and that the contact routes are current. For SHUJAN no contact page was verified. TIB's pages show differing telephone numbers across sources, so use the contact page above, not the numbers in third-party listings.
- The Bengali draft was written by an AI and needs review by a native speaker.
- Do not describe either organisation as a supporter. Neither has expressed a view on the demand.
