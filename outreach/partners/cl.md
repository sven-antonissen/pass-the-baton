# Chile (CL): potential partner organisations

Status: **draft for maintainer review. Nothing has been sent.**
Researched: 2026-10-13. Every organisation below has its own website found in this run. Only organisational, public contact routes are listed.

Context: Chile is an open democracy where civil-society and student activism is common, so there is no special safety concern for outreach. Politics is polarised, though. The movement is non-partisan, so the first contact should be careful not to appear tied to any camp.

### Considered but not listed

- **CONFECH** (Confederación de Estudiantes de Chile). The national student confederation is clearly active: news reports from late September and October 2026 cover its national strike and march. Its website (confechoficial.cl) was only seen in a Wikipedia entry, not verified on the site itself in this run. A maintainer could verify the site and consider it as a student-union contact. Note that it is currently in a public dispute with the government over the 2027 education budget, so it is strongly engaged in current politics.
- **Fundación Democracia y Desarrollo** (fdd.cl). Works on citizen participation, with a focus on younger generations. The pages seen in this run showed material mostly from around 2016, so I could not verify that it is currently active. It was founded by a former president, which is another reason to check before contact.
- Think tanks that openly follow a party-political or ideological line were not selected, to keep a balanced list.

## Draft e-mail (English)

Subject: Introducing "Pass the Baton": would you like to talk?

Dear team at [organisation],

I am writing on behalf of Pass the Baton, a global, non-violent and non-partisan movement at an early stage. Its demand is simple: anyone who has reached the statutory retirement age of their own country, and at the latest 67, does not start a new term in one of the highest offices.

We are not against older people. Experience stays valuable as advice, mentoring and diplomacy. We ask only that the same norm of renewal that applies to citizens also applies to those who lead them. It applies to every country and every party equally. A term already under way may be completed.

Our manifesto is here: https://pass-the-baton.org/manifesto/

Your work on [transparency / participation / democracy / intergenerational dialogue] made us think you might find this topic worth discussing, even if you end up disagreeing with it. We do not assume that you support us. Would you be open to a short conversation, or to telling us what you think? If it is not a topic for your organisation, we fully understand.

With kind regards,
[name], Pass the Baton

## Maintainer checks before contacting

- Confirm each organisation's current contact page or general address on its own site. For Ciudadanía Inteligente, Conecta Mayor and Las Rosas no dedicated contact page was confirmed in this run. Use only general or organisational addresses.
- Chile Transparente's page also lists a named press contact. That person's details were deliberately **not** copied here. Use only the general address.
- Tailor the bracketed topic in the e-mail to each organisation. Do not send the same text to all of them.
- Espacio Público is often described in the press as close to the centre-left, and Conecta Mayor is part of a Catholic university. Mention nothing that suggests the movement leans to any camp.
- Check the Chilean statutory retirement age(s) that the Chilean version of the demand should cite (I believe it differs by sex, but I have not verified this in this run), and check the country profile `data/countries/cl.json` for the current office-holders before naming anyone. This draft names no individual.
- Do not describe the movement as large. The draft says "early stage"; keep it that way unless the numbers justify more.
