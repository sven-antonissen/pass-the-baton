## Draft e-mail (English)

**Subject:** Pass the Baton: a question about renewal in leadership

Dear [organisation name] team,

I am writing on behalf of Pass the Baton, a non-violent and non-partisan movement that is still at an early stage. [One sentence on why we are writing to this organisation, e.g. its work on civic education.]

Our demand is simple: anyone who has reached the statutory retirement age of their own country, and at the latest 67, does not start a new term in one of the highest offices. A term already under way may be completed, and ordinary citizens stay free to keep working. We are not against older people; their experience remains valuable as advisers and mentors. We simply think the same age norm should apply to those who lead as to the citizens they serve.

Our manifesto is here: https://pass-the-baton.org/manifesto/

We are not asking you to endorse anything. We would simply like to know whether you would be open to a short conversation, and we would welcome your honest view, including criticism.

Thank you for your time.

Kind regards,
[name], Pass the Baton

## Maintainer checks before contacting

- The country profile (`data/countries/at.json`) was not part of this run's input. Please check that the local retirement age and the current office holders are described correctly before anything is sent, and adapt the e-mail to the Austrian pension age if you want a local reference.
- Confirm each organisation is still active and that the general address is current; for PolEdu, ÖGPB and Generationen Dialog no contact page was verified, so look for the contact route on the website first.
- Do not describe any of them as supporters. None has expressed a view on the demand.
- Keep the framing party-neutral. Several of these organisations are explicitly party-independent and will react badly to anything that looks aimed at a particular politician or party.
- Fridays For Future Austria: its imprint says the registered association is only a small financial part of the movement, so the address is for press and cooperation requests, not for the whole movement.
- Jung & Alt and Generationen Dialog work on care and encounter between generations, not on politics. Keep the tone modest and expect that they may decline.
- Considered but left out: the Bundesjugendvertretung (BJV), the national youth council. It is established by law and also represents party youth organisations, so a maintainer should decide whether it counts as an organisation under our rules. Its website is https://bjv.at/ and its general contact page is https://bjv.at/ueber-die-bjv/kontakt/. Zentrum polis was left out because it is commissioned by the Ministry of Education.
