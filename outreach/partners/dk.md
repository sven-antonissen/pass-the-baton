## Draft e-mail (English)

**Subject:** Would you like to talk? Pass the Baton, an age norm for the highest offices

Dear [organisation name],

I am writing on behalf of Pass the Baton, a global, non-violent and non-partisan movement. We are a new initiative at an early stage.

Our demand is simple: anyone who has reached the statutory retirement age of their own country, and at the latest 67, does not start a new term in one of the highest offices. A term already under way may be completed. We have nothing against older people. Their experience stays valuable as adviser, mentor or ambassador. The norm applies to everyone, left or right.

Our manifesto is here: https://pass-the-baton.org/manifesto/

We do not know whether this topic falls within your work or mandate, and we do not expect you to take a position. We would simply like to ask whether you would be open to a conversation: to hear your views, including criticism, and to learn from your experience. If not, we fully understand and will not write again.

Kind regards,
[name], Pass the Baton

## Draft e-mail (Danish / dansk)

**Emne:** Har I lyst til en samtale? Pass the Baton, en aldersnorm for de højeste embeder

Kære [organisationens navn],

Jeg skriver på vegne af Pass the Baton, en global, ikke-voldelig og partipolitisk uafhængig bevægelse. Vi er et nyt initiativ i en tidlig fase.

Vores krav er enkelt: Den, der har nået pensionsalderen i sit eget land, og senest 67 år, starter ikke en ny periode i et af de højeste embeder. En periode, der allerede er i gang, kan fuldføres. Vi har ikke noget imod ældre mennesker. Deres erfaring er fortsat værdifuld som rådgiver, mentor eller ambassadør. Normen gælder for alle, uanset om man er til højre eller venstre.

Vores manifest kan læses her: https://pass-the-baton.org/manifesto/

Vi ved ikke, om emnet ligger inden for jeres arbejde eller formål, og vi forventer ikke, at I tager stilling. Vi vil blot gerne høre, om I har lyst til en uforpligtende samtale, så vi kan høre jeres synspunkter, også kritiske, og lære af jeres erfaring. Hvis ikke, har vi fuld forståelse, og vi skriver ikke igen.

Venlig hilsen
[name], Pass the Baton

## Maintainer checks before contacting

- **Retirement age.** Check the figure in `data/countries/dk.json` against current Danish law. I did not verify it in this run. As far as I know the statutory age has been set to rise over time, so the "at the latest 67" ceiling may be the binding part of the demand. Describe it accurately in any follow-up.
- **Contact routes.** The Transparency International Danmark and Demokrativærnet contact routes were not confirmed to a specific general address in this run. Use the contact form or general address on their own sites. Do not use the personal addresses of staff or board members that some of the other contact pages list.
- **DGS.** Its members are mostly minors. Keep to the general address and do not approach pupils directly. Consider whether a first contact should go to the organisation's own leadership via the general inbox only.
- **Ældre Sagen.** Representing older people's interests, it may read an age norm as age discrimination. Tailor the tone, stress that ordinary citizens stay free to keep working, and be ready for a polite no.
- **DUF.** Its member organisations include political youth organisations. We contact DUF only, not those members, and keep our non-partisan stance clear.
- None of these organisations has said it supports the movement. Do not suggest otherwise, and do not state a size for the movement beyond what is true at the time of sending.
- Not included because they could not be sufficiently verified or were out of scope: political parties and their youth wings, and the municipal "Ungdommens Demokratihus" (run by Copenhagen Municipality, so a government body).
