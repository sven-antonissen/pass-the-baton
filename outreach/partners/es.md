Status: **draft for maintainer review. Nothing has been sent.**

## Draft e-mail (English)

**Subject:** Would you like to talk? A proposal on age limits for the highest offices

Dear team at [organisation],

I am writing on behalf of Pass the Baton, a global, non-violent and non-partisan movement. Our demand is simple: anyone who has reached the statutory retirement age of their own country, and at the latest 67, does not start a new term in one of the highest offices. A term already under way can be completed. We are not against older people. We ask for the same norm for leaders as for the citizens they serve, and we see passing the baton as an act of leadership.

Our manifesto is here: https://pass-the-baton.org/manifesto/

We know your organisation works on [youth participation / good governance / transparency / the interests of older people], and we wanted to ask whether you would be open to a short conversation. We are not asking you to endorse anything. We would simply like to hear your view and learn from your experience in Spain.

Thank you for your time. We understand if this is not a fit for you.

With kind regards,
[name], Pass the Baton

## Maintainer checks before contacting

- Check the Spanish retirement-age figures and the offices in scope against `data/countries/es.json` before mentioning any specific number or office in a follow-up. The draft deliberately names none.
- The CJE is a statutory platform created by law (a LinkedIn listing even labels it a government agency). It is made up of youth associations, so it is probably an organisation, but please decide whether it counts under the "no government bodies" rule. It also has a member structure that includes trade-union and political youth wings, so the contact may be slow and cautious.
- Hay Derecho has taken legal action over public appointments. Civio and Political Watch publish critical work on authorities. Check that our message cannot be read as aligned with any party, and avoid naming current Spanish leaders.
- CEOMA represents older people. Make sure the tone stays respectful and the "not against older people" principle is up front.
- Transparency International España's contact page mainly describes an ethics-code address. The general address used here (comunicacion@) comes from the footer of its own site. Confirm it is the right inbox.
- Replace the bracketed placeholders and personalise the draft. Do not add staff names or personal addresses.
