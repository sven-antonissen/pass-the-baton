# Contributing

Thank you for wanting to help pass the baton. There is room for everyone, coder or not.

## Ways to help

- **Translate.** Add the manifesto in your language under `translations/<language-code>/` (for example `translations/fr/MANIFESTO.md`). Adapt it to your country, don't just translate it word for word.
- **Research.** Help build country profiles: retirement age, the age of the highest office holders, political system. Every fact needs a source.
- **Build.** The agents, the website and the approval screen are built here in the open. Look for issues labelled `good first issue`.
- **Start a local chapter.** Open an issue with the label `chapter` and tell us where you are.
- **Improve the texts.** Clearer, shorter, stronger: proposals welcome.

## How

1. Open an issue first for anything larger than a typo, so we can agree on the direction.
2. Fork the repository, make your change on a separate branch and open a pull request.
3. Keep changes small and focused. One pull request, one topic.
4. Be ready for feedback; every change to public texts is reviewed by a human before it's merged.

## Ground rules

- Follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
- No fake accounts, no astroturfing, no unsolicited mass messaging, ever. Contributions that weaken the [safeguards](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md#limits-and-safeguards) will not be merged.
- Never commit keys, passwords or personal data.
- By contributing, you agree that code is published under [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE) and texts under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
